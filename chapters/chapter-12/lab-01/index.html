Key concepts

Gradients store energyThe Na⁺/K⁺ pump keeps Na⁺ high outside and K⁺ high inside. Opening a channel lets an ion flow down its electrochemical gradient.
Graded vs. action potentialsSynaptic (graded) potentials vary in size and fade with distance. Action potentials are all-or-none and are regenerated as they travel.
ThresholdAt about −55 mV, enough voltage-gated Na⁺ channels open that Na⁺ entry outpaces K⁺ exit, and depolarization becomes self-reinforcing.
Refractory periodsNa⁺ channel inactivation makes the membrane briefly unable to fire again, so signals travel one way and stay separate.
Conduction velocityLarger axon diameter and myelin both speed conduction. Myelin is the bigger effect: it lets the signal jump between nodes.
