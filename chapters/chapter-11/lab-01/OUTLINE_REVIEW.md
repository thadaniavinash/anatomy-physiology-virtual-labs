# Muscle outline drafts: review list (November 2026)

Outlines live in `data/muscle-regions.json`; each side of a muscle owns a set of model triangles. All were drafted
automatically (texture-aware fill from the labelling point or from points chosen in rendered views, copied to the
other side across the midline) and checked in close-up renders. Clicking each of the 70 outlined sides from
outside the model identifies the right muscle and side.

**They are drafts: check each boundary anatomically** and correct it in Author mode (Fill / Brush / Erase /
Copy to other side; see README).
